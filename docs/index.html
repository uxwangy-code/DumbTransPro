Questions

I don't have an API key. Is that hard?

No. Zhipu GLM gives you a free model (glm-4-flash) just for signing up — that's the default we recommend. Any OpenAI-compatible key works too. Setup is: paste key, done.

Is my text private?

Your selected text goes directly from your Mac to the AI provider you configured, using your key. The app has no servers and collects nothing. The only network calls we make are license verification (Gumroad) and update checks. Details in the privacy policy.

How many Macs can I use a license on?

Your personal Macs — work laptop and home machine are both fine. Licenses are per person, not per device.

What if it's not for me?

Email us within 14 days for a full refund, no questions asked. See the refund policy.

Why "DumbTrans"?

Because the Chinglish mode translates 加油 as "add-oil" and we refuse to apologize for it.
